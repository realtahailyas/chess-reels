Drop your own background music here.

Supported: .mp3 .wav .m4a .ogg .flac .aac .opus

One track is chosen per video by hashing the puzzle id, so consecutive posts do
not reuse the same bed. Tracks are trimmed or looped to the video length and
faded in/out automatically — length does not matter.

Leave this folder empty (this file is ignored) and the pipeline generates a
lo-fi bed instead, with the key and BPM rotating per video.
